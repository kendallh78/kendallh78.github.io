About

My interest in statistics and machine learning led me to pursue a B.S. in Data Science and an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Worcester Polytechnic Institute.

I enjoy understanding patterns in data, experimenting with models, and finding ways to turn technical ideas into practical solutions. That interest has carried into my professional experience, where I've worked across machine learning workflows, full-stack application development, and production systems.

I'm particularly drawn to opportunities that combine analytical problem-solving with hands-on engineering, whether that means developing models, building AI-powered applications, or working with others to solve complex technical challenges.

In my previous role, I helped develop enterprise machine learning applications, working across data preprocessing, clustering workflows, full-stack interfaces, and backend integrations.

I developed reusable feature transformations, designed configurable ML workflows, and built user-facing features for complex modeling routines. I collaborated across teams to validate simulations and port workflows across application versions. I also investigated and resolved production issues involving Azure, application services, and SQL Server.
